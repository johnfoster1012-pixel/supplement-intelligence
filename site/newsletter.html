Graded Claims is a free weekly email from Supplement Intelligence. Each week we take a few supplement, GLP-1 and peptide claims you will see online and check them against the research. Plain English, sources linked, not medical advice.

What do you get in each issue?

  • 3–5 claims a week, graded. Each claim gets a short verdict and links to the studies behind it.
  • An A, B or C grade on every claim. A means strong human trial evidence for the outcome claimed; B means smaller or less direct evidence; C means animal, lab or mechanism evidence, or comparisons between separate trials.
  • No sponsors in the grades. Grades come from the studies. Any sponsor or referral link is labeled and never changes a grade.

How often is Graded Claims sent?

Once a week. Each issue grades 3–5 health claims you are likely to see online.

What do the A, B and C grades mean?

Grade A means the claim is backed by large randomized trials in people for the outcome claimed. Grade B means the evidence is smaller, earlier or less direct, such as Phase 2 trials. Grade C means the claim rests on animal or lab studies, mechanism, or comparisons between separate trials.

Is Graded Claims medical advice?

No. It summarizes published research in plain English. It does not tell anyone what to take, how much, or whether to start or stop a medicine.

Do sponsors affect the grades?

No. Grades come from the studies. If an issue ever includes a sponsor or referral link, it will be labeled, and it will not change any grade.
