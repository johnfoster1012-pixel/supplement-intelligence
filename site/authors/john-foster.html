John Foster

John Foster founded and edits Supplement Intelligence (also called Supplement Intel). He writes the site's evidence guides and grades each claim A, B or C based on how strong the human evidence is in the primary papers. He is not a doctor, pharmacist or dietitian, and the pages are not personal medical advice.

How John checks a page

  • Every figure comes from a primary source, such as an FDA label, a randomized trial or a systematic review, and is checked against that source before the page goes live.
  • Each claim gets a grade (A, B or C) for how strong the human evidence is for that exact outcome. How evidence is reviewed.
  • Pages say when they were last reviewed, and what is not known.
  • Pages have not been reviewed by a medically qualified person unless they say so.
