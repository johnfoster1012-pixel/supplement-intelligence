About Supplement Intelligence

Supplement Intelligence is a research and product-information project focused on making supplement formulation data and supporting evidence easier to inspect.

Editorial standard

We distinguish product-label facts from ingredient-level research and from evidence about a finished formulation. When a label, dose, citation, or study-to-claim match has not been verified, we mark it under review rather than presenting it as established.

Commercial disclosure

Supplement Intelligence may earn referral credit when users purchase through certain links, including links to Vital Health Global. This financial relationship is disclosed because it is material to readers evaluating the site.

Research status

Historical citation collections that did not meet the current verification standard have been withdrawn. The database is being rebuilt using primary sources and explicit evidence-quality fields.

Medical disclaimer

Content is for informational purposes only and is not medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before using supplements.
