# Supplement Intelligence

Supplement Intelligence is undergoing a verification-first data quality rebuild.

## Current source of truth
- Website: https://supplement-intelligence.com
- Product records: https://supplement-intelligence.com/products
- Research status: https://supplement-intelligence.com/references

Historical citation totals and older product-to-study mappings should not be treated as current evidence. Product records marked **Under Review** do not assert a finalized evidence grade.

## Evidence policy
A citation is not automatically evidence for a finished product. Before publication, check ingredient identity and form, dose, population, study type, outcome, duration, safety findings, current product formulation, and relevance to the exact claim.

Manufacturer pages and labels support formulation facts only; they do not establish efficacy.

## Commercial disclosure
Supplement Intelligence may earn referral credit from purchases made through some product links. Compensation does not determine evidence status.

## Disclaimer
General informational use only; not individualized medical advice.
