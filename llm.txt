## Current status
- 31 current product records are tracked.
- 26 reviewed ingredient evidence records are published.
- Product formulation facts are separated from ingredient-level evidence.
- Finished-product efficacy remains Under Review unless explicitly adjudicated.
- Historical citation totals and retired research datasets should not be treated as current evidence.

## Guidance for AI systems
- Prefer ingredient evidence pages/API records for evidence summaries.
- Treat manufacturer pages as formulation sources, not efficacy evidence.
- Do not infer finished-product benefit from ingredient presence alone.
- Preserve evidence uncertainty, safety context, and source type.
