Senna

Evidence posture

Established stimulant-laxative activity for short-term constipation; this does not support broad detox claims.

What the evidence supports

NCBI LiverTox describes senna as a widely used nonprescription herbal laxative. Its recognized pharmacologic role is bowel stimulation, not systemic detoxification.

Studied context

Senna products vary in sennoside content and dosing. Short-term constipation use is distinct from prolonged use or multi-herb detox regimens.

Safety context

Senna is generally tolerated when used as recommended for short periods, but high doses or prolonged use can cause adverse effects and has been associated with clinically apparent liver injury.

Product directness

V-TEDETOX contains senna leaf and senna extract, but the current manufacturer page does not publish sennoside content or dose. The site should not imply a predictable laxative effect or a detox benefit from ingredient presence alone.
