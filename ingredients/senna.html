Senna / sennosides

Evidence posture

Established stimulant laxative for short-term constipation; this does not establish a 'detox' benefit.

What the evidence supports

MedlinePlus identifies senna as a stimulant laxative used short term for constipation and bowel evacuation. It increases intestinal activity to produce a bowel movement, typically within 6–12 hours.

Studied context

Evidence and regulated OTC use relate to laxation/constipation, not removal of toxins, liver detoxification, or chronic digestive cleansing.

Safety context

MedlinePlus advises not using senna for more than one week without medical guidance. Cramping, nausea, stomach discomfort, dependence with frequent/continued use, and medication interactions are important considerations.

Product directness

V-TEDETOX contains senna leaf and senna extract, but the manufacturer page does not provide sennoside dose. The evidence supports a laxative mechanism, not the broader 'detox' positioning.
