Milk thistle (Silybum marianum)

Evidence reviewed: 2026-10-02

Evidence posture

Insufficient high-quality evidence for liver-disease benefits; broad liver-detox claims are not supported.

What the evidence supports

Systematic reviews of randomized trials have not shown reliable improvements in mortality, liver histology, or clinically meaningful liver-function outcomes. Evidence remains insufficient to support broad liver-treatment or detox claims.

Studied context

Milk-thistle extracts differ in silymarin content and composition. Evidence from a standardized extract should not be generalized to unspecified milk-thistle powder.

Safety context

Oral use is usually well tolerated, with digestive symptoms most common. Allergic reactions and supplement-quality problems can occur, and drug interactions are possible.

Product directness

V-ORGANEX and V-TEDETOX list milk thistle, but the current manufacturer pages do not publish silymarin standardization or dose. Liver-support efficacy remains unverified.
