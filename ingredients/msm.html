Methylsulfonylmethane (MSM)

Evidence posture

Limited human evidence suggests possible short-term knee-pain/function benefit, but the evidence is positive rather than definitive and long-term efficacy/safety remain uncertain.

What the evidence supports

Small randomized trials have reported improvements in pain or function in knee osteoarthritis at gram-level daily doses. A systematic review concluded that the more rigorous MSM trials were positive but not definitive, and further efficacy and long-term safety studies were needed. A trial in military trainees did not show preventive benefit for knee pain.

Studied context

Positive trials used gram-level MSM dosing for roughly 8–12 weeks. Different combination products and lower doses should not be assumed equivalent.

Safety context

Short-term trials generally report tolerability, but long-term safety data are limited. Evidence from reviews may include industry-linked authors, which should be considered when interpreting certainty.

Product directness

V-ASCULAX, V-CURCUMAX, V-DAILY, V-ITADOL, and V-DAILY SACHET list MSM, but current manufacturer pages do not publish MSM dose. The site should not imply osteoarthritis benefit from presence alone.
