Berberine

Evidence posture

Supportive but heterogeneous evidence for glycemic and lipid outcomes in type 2 diabetes; weight-loss evidence is inconclusive.

What the evidence supports

NCCIH reports some evidence that berberine may improve blood glucose, insulin resistance, and lipid measures in people with type 2 diabetes, but study quality, populations, formulations, and effects vary. Evidence for weight loss is not conclusive.

Studied context

Published studies use varied formulations and doses, often in people with diabetes or other metabolic disease. Results should not be generalized to all users or all products.

Safety context

Common adverse effects are gastrointestinal. Berberine can interact with medicines and should not be used during pregnancy or breastfeeding or given to infants.

Product directness

S-BALANCE and NOURISH+ list berberine, but current manufacturer pages do not establish a trial-equivalent berberine dose. Product-level glucose or weight-loss claims therefore remain unverified.
