Echinacea

Evidence posture

May slightly reduce the chance of developing a cold; evidence that it shortens cold duration is unclear.

What the evidence supports

NCCIH reports that taking echinacea may slightly reduce the chance of catching a cold, but it remains unclear whether it shortens the duration of a cold. Evidence for other promoted uses is insufficient.

Studied context

Echinacea species, plant parts, extraction methods, and preparations vary substantially across studies.

Safety context

Short-term use is likely safe for most adults, but allergic reactions can occur and may be severe. Digestive symptoms are common; interactions with immunosuppressants, caffeine, and some liver-metabolized drugs are possible.

Product directness

V-ITALBOOST lists echinacea but does not publish species, extract type, or dose. The product should not be described as preventing or treating respiratory infections based on echinacea alone.
