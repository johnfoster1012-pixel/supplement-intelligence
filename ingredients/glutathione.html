Oral glutathione

Evidence posture

Bioavailability is formulation- and duration-dependent; biomarker changes are documented, but clinical health-outcome benefits remain uncertain.

What the evidence supports

Human studies have produced different findings. An older single-dose study found negligible systemic availability, while a 6-month randomized trial reported increased glutathione stores, and newer pharmacokinetic research suggests formulation-dependent absorption.

Studied context

Dose, formulation, duration, and outcome matter. Raising a blood or tissue glutathione marker is not itself proof of a clinical benefit.

Safety context

Short-term studies generally report tolerability, but the evidence base for long-term clinical outcomes and formulation-specific safety is limited.

Product directness

V-GLUTATION lists glutathione but does not provide a dose or formulation specification on the current manufacturer page. Clinical benefit claims should remain under review.
