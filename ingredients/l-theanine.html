L-theanine

Evidence posture

Possible small acute cognitive/mood benefits, especially when combined with caffeine; magnitude and clinical relevance remain uncertain.

What the evidence supports

A 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis of randomized trials found small-to-moderate differences favoring L-theanine plus caffeine for selected attention, reaction-time, and mood outcomes, with frequent uncertainty in confidence intervals. L-theanine alone showed limited signals for some outcomes.

Studied context

Effects in the literature are acute and task-specific, and often studied in combination with caffeine. Dose ratios and timing vary.

Safety context

Short-term trial data generally suggest tolerability, but evidence is not sufficient to support broad therapeutic claims or long-term cognitive-health claims.

Product directness

V-NRGY lists L-theanine together with caffeine-containing ingredients, but the manufacturer page does not publish an L-theanine dose or caffeine-to-theanine ratio. Study effects cannot be directly assigned to the finished product.
