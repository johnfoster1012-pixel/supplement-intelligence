L-citrulline / citrulline malate

Evidence posture

Evidence for exercise performance is mixed; recent meta-analyses do not show robust improvements in aerobic performance or maximal strength.

What the evidence supports

A systematic review/meta-analysis found no significant overall benefit of citrulline on aerobic performance, perceived exertion, oxygen kinetics, or lactate. A separate meta-analysis found no overall strength benefit from citrulline malate in resistance-trained adults. Some acute studies suggest possible effects on repetition performance, but results are not consistent enough for a broad performance claim.

Studied context

Citrulline and citrulline malate are not interchangeable; trials use different amounts, malate ratios, and timing. Acute protocols are common.

Safety context

Short-term studies generally report good tolerability, but the safety of higher-dose long-term use is less established.

Product directness

V-NITRO lists L-citrulline but does not publish dose or whether it is free L-citrulline versus a citrulline-malate protocol. Trial outcomes cannot be directly assigned to the product.
