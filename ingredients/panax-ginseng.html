Panax ginseng

Evidence posture

Human evidence is mixed and outcome-specific; small signals exist for fatigue and some cognitive outcomes, while athletic-performance evidence is largely negative.

What the evidence supports

NCCIH reports small or preliminary evidence for some cognitive measures in middle-aged adults and a small possible effect on general fatigue, but notes that research on blood-sugar control is conflicting and that most research does not show improved athletic performance.

Studied context

Trials use different preparations, ginsenoside concentrations, doses, and populations. 'Panax ginseng' on an ingredient list is not enough to establish equivalence to a studied extract.

Safety context

Short-term oral use up to about 6 months appears reasonably tolerated for most people. Insomnia is common; serious allergic reactions and liver injury are rare. Ginseng may affect blood sugar, clotting, autoimmune conditions, and medications.

Product directness

V-LOVKAFE, V-NEUROKAFE, and V-THERMOKAFE list Panax ginseng, but current manufacturer pages do not publish extract standardization or dose. Claims about fatigue, cognition, sexual function, or performance remain indirect.
