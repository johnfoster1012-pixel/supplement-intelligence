Panax ginseng (Asian ginseng)

Evidence posture

Limited human evidence suggests possible small benefits for fatigue and selected cognitive measures; broad adaptogen, performance, or immune claims are not established.

What the evidence supports

NCCIH reports a small amount of evidence for selected cognitive effects in middle-aged adults and a possible small effect on general fatigue, while emphasizing that most trials are small and short.

Studied context

Ginseng preparations differ substantially in extraction method and ginsenoside content. Findings from one preparation should not be generalized to an unspecified ginseng extract.

Safety context

Short-term oral use appears generally tolerated for many adults, but insomnia is common. NCCIH notes possible drug interactions, blood-clotting concerns, blood-sugar effects, autoimmune concerns, and pregnancy cautions.

Product directness

V-LOVKAFE, V-NEUROKAFE, and V THERMOKAFE list Panax ginseng, but the current manufacturer pages do not publish extract standardization or dose. Trial findings cannot be assigned directly to these products.
