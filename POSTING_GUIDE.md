# Publishing guide

Do not publish or syndicate a product claim until the current product formulation and supporting evidence have passed the verification method in `docs/EVIDENCE_METHOD.md`.

## Per-topic workflow
1. Verify the current manufacturer label and record the verification date.
2. Verify every citation against its primary bibliographic record.
3. Classify evidence by study type and directness.
4. Publish the canonical page on supplement-intelligence.com.
5. Add a clear commercial disclosure if a page includes referral links.
6. Share educational summaries externally without overstating certainty.
7. Recheck the label and evidence when the formulation changes or important new evidence appears.

## Do not
- use invented expert identities or credentials
- treat ingredient evidence as finished-product evidence
- claim GRADE without performing formal GRADE assessment
- repeat unsupported claims across platforms to create apparent consensus
- publish numerical citation totals unless the set is current and verified
