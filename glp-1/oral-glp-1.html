Oral GLP-1 pills: what the trials actually show

Prescription oral GLP-1 medicines include oral semaglutide and orforglipron. They are a different category from dietary supplements marketed as GLP-1 pills, boosters, or natural GLP-1. Published trials show weight and blood-sugar effects in specific populations, but trial amounts, commercial tablet strengths, and approved indications must be kept separate.

Frequently asked questions

Are “GLP-1 pills” the same as GLP-1 supplements?
No. This page covers prescription oral medicines. Products sold as GLP-1 supplements or boosters are a different category and are reviewed separately.

Which oral GLP-1 medicines are FDA-approved in the United States?
As of the labels reviewed, Wegovy tablets (oral semaglutide, with a labeled maintenance amount of 25 mg once daily) are indicated for weight reduction and for reducing certain cardiovascular event risks in eligible adults. Foundayo (orforglipron) is indicated for weight reduction and maintenance in adults with obesity, or overweight plus a weight-related condition. Rybelsus is oral semaglutide studied and used for type 2 diabetes at 3, 7, and 14 mg. [DailyMed labels; PMID 31186300]

How much weight did oral semaglutide 25 mg lose in a trial?
In OASIS 4, average weight change at week 64 was −13.6% with oral semaglutide 25 mg versus −2.2% with placebo, a difference of −11.4 percentage points, in adults with overweight or obesity without diabetes. [PMID 40934115]

What did orforglipron show for weight in people without diabetes?
In ATTAIN-1, 72-week average weight change was −7.5%, −8.4%, and −11.2% with the 6, 12, and 36 mg trial groups versus −2.1% with placebo. Trial milligram amounts are not the same as the commercial tablet strengths on the Foundayo label. [PMID 40960239; Foundayo label]

Do label directions differ for food?
Yes. The Wegovy tablet label directs taking the tablet once daily in the morning on an empty stomach with water (up to 4 ounces), then waiting at least 30 minutes before food, other drinks, or other oral medicines. The Foundayo label directs taking the tablet once daily with or without food. Those are labeling instructions, not personal advice. [DailyMed]

How did orforglipron compare with oral semaglutide in type 2 diabetes?
In ACHIEVE-3, both orforglipron trial doses lowered HbA1c more than oral semaglutide 7 mg and 14 mg at 52 weeks. Gastrointestinal events, discontinuations, and the average pulse-rate rise were higher with orforglipron. [PMID 41765029]

How to read the evidence

  • Grade A: published Phase 3 randomized trials for reported outcomes.
  • Grade B: Phase 2, exploratory, nested, post hoc, observational, or labeling evidence.
  • Grade C: uncontrolled reports, mechanistic predictions, and unmatched separate-trial comparisons.

Prescription medicines and supplements are different categories

This page covers prescription medicines. The GLP-1 supplements review covers a different category; prescription trial results cannot be assigned to those products.

What the reviewed labels cover

The Wegovy tablet label covers long-term weight reduction and maintenance in eligible adults and reduction of major cardiovascular events in eligible adults with established cardiovascular disease. Its labeled maintenance amount is 25 mg once daily. The label specifies an empty stomach in the morning, up to 4 ounces of water, and waiting at least 30 minutes before food, drinks, or oral medicines. These are label facts, not personal instructions.

Foundayo labeling covers weight reduction and maintenance in eligible adults. It specifies once-daily administration with or without food and tablets swallowed whole. The retrieved US label does not give a type 2 diabetes indication. Trial milligram amounts are not commercial tablet strengths. Rybelsus is oral semaglutide used for type 2 diabetes. [DailyMed; PMID 31186300]

Oral semaglutide for weight: OASIS trials

OASIS 4: the 25 mg trial

At week 64, mean weight change was −13.6% with 25 mg oral semaglutide versus −2.2% with placebo, a difference of −11.4 percentage points. Gastrointestinal adverse events occurred in 74.0% versus 42.2%. The trial randomized 307 participants. [PMID 40934115]

OASIS 1 and OASIS 2: 50 mg research protocols

OASIS 1 found −15.1% versus −2.4% at 68 weeks. OASIS 2 found −14.3% versus −1.3% in East Asian participants. Both studied 50 mg, not the labeled US 25 mg maintenance amount. Gastrointestinal events occurred in 80% versus 46% in OASIS 1 and 63.4% versus 34.8% in OASIS 2. Grade A. [PMIDs 37385278, 40758358]

Oral semaglutide for diabetes: PIONEER 1

Among 703 people with type 2 diabetes, placebo-adjusted HbA1c differences at 26 weeks were −0.6, −0.9, and −1.1 percentage points for 3, 7, and 14 mg; weight differences were −0.1, −0.9, and −2.3 kg. These are diabetes outcomes, not an obesity-dose comparison. [PMID 31186300]

Orforglipron for weight: ATTAIN trials

ATTAIN-1 enrolled 3,127 adults without diabetes. At 72 weeks, mean weight changes were −7.5%, −8.4%, and −11.2% with 6, 12, and 36 mg versus −2.1% with placebo. Adverse-event discontinuations were 5.3–10.3% versus 2.7%. Grade A. [PMID 40960239]

ATTAIN-2 enrolled 1,613 participants with type 2 diabetes. Weight changes were −5.1%, −7.0%, and −9.6% versus −2.5%. Ten deaths were reported; this page does not infer causality. Grade A trial outcomes, not a US diabetes approval. [PMID 41275875]

Orforglipron for diabetes: ACHIEVE trials

In ACHIEVE-1 (559 participants, 40 weeks; 3, 12, and 36 mg), HbA1c changes were −1.24, −1.47, and −1.48 versus −0.41 percentage points, and weight changes of −4.5%, −5.8%, and −7.6% versus −1.7%. Grade A trial evidence, not an approved US diabetes indication. [PMID 40544435]

ACHIEVE-3 compared orforglipron 12 or 36 mg with oral semaglutide 7 or 14 mg. HbA1c changes were −1.71 and −1.91 versus −1.23 and −1.47 percentage points. Gastrointestinal events, discontinuations, and pulse-rate increases were higher with orforglipron. It did not compare semaglutide 25 mg. [PMID 41765029]

Boxed warnings need precise wording

Wegovy carries a boxed warning about rodent thyroid C-cell tumors; human relevance is unknown. Foundayo also carries a boxed warning, but orforglipron is not pharmacologically active in rats or mice and did not produce tumors in rodents. Both labels include contraindications relating to medullary thyroid carcinoma and MEN 2. [DailyMed labels]

Bottom line

Prescription oral GLP-1 medicines have trial and label evidence that does not apply to supplement boosters. Studies differ in population, comparator, duration, and amount. Separate obesity trials do not establish a direct drug ranking; trial results do not expand approved indications or convert trial amounts into commercial strengths.
