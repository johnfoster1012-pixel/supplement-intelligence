Tirzepatide side effects: what trials and labels show

Short answer. Tirzepatide commonly causes stomach and bowel side effects, and its label also describes serious risks. This is not a complete list and is not medical advice. The FDA label and a clinician are the sources for individual safety decisions.

Frequently asked questions

What are the most common tirzepatide side effects?
Stomach and bowel problems are common. In the pooled Zepbound weight-reduction trials, nausea occurred in 25%, 29%, and 28% at the studied 5, 10, and 15 mg doses versus 8% with placebo. Diarrhea, vomiting, and constipation were also common. [Zepbound label]

Does tirzepatide cause hair loss?
Hair loss was reported and was associated with weight reduction in the Zepbound label. Rates were 5%, 4%, and 5% at the studied 5, 10, and 15 mg doses versus 1% with placebo. It was reported more often in women. [Zepbound label]

Does tirzepatide cause muscle loss?
Lean mass fell in the SURMOUNT-1 DXA substudy: −10.9% with pooled tirzepatide versus −2.6% with placebo at week 72. About 75% of weight lost was fat mass and 25% was lean mass in both groups. These body-composition results do not establish that tirzepatide preserves or destroys muscle. [PMID 39996356]

What serious risks appear on the FDA label?
The label includes a boxed thyroid C-cell tumor warning and warnings about severe gastrointestinal reactions, dehydration-related kidney injury, gallbladder disease, pancreatitis, serious allergy, low blood sugar, diabetic retinopathy complications, and pulmonary aspiration during anesthesia or deep sedation. This is not a complete list. [Zepbound label]

Is tirzepatide FDA-approved, and for what?
Yes. Mounjaro labeling covers blood-sugar control in type 2 diabetes at ages 10+ and cardiovascular-event risk reduction in adults with type 2 diabetes at high risk. Zepbound labeling covers weight reduction and maintenance in adults with obesity or overweight plus a weight-related condition, and moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity. [Mounjaro and Zepbound labels]

Do side effects go away after the dose is raised?
Not for everyone. The Zepbound label reports that most nausea, vomiting, and/or diarrhea occurred during dose escalation and decreased over time. That is a trial observation, not a promise for an individual. [Zepbound label]

How to read the evidence

  • Grade A: a published Phase 3 randomized trial, or for nutrient pages a published systematic review of randomized trials, for outcomes reported within that evidence.
  • Grade B: a Phase 2 trial, exploratory or subgroup analysis, small human trial, observational study, or labeling statement beyond a trial abstract.
  • Grade C: uncontrolled reports, animal or laboratory inference, mechanism-based predictions, and comparisons between separate trials.

What tirzepatide is

Tirzepatide is a weekly GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist. Mounjaro and Zepbound have different labeled uses. The label does not recommend coadministration with other tirzepatide products or any GLP-1 receptor agonist.

Gastrointestinal adverse events overall occurred in 56% at each studied dose versus 30% with placebo. Most gastrointestinal discontinuations occurred in the first few months.

Boxed warning and contraindications

In rats, tirzepatide causes thyroid C-cell tumors at clinically relevant exposures. It is unknown whether it causes these tumors in humans. The label contraindicates use with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2, and with known serious hypersensitivity.

Other serious warnings

Severe gut reactions and kidney injury

The label does not recommend Zepbound in severe gastroparesis. Acute kidney injury due to volume depletion has been reported, often after nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea.

Gallbladder disease and pancreatitis

The label warns about acute gallbladder disease and pancreatitis, including fatal and non-fatal hemorrhagic or necrotizing pancreatitis.

Allergy, low blood sugar, eye complications, and procedures

Serious allergy has been reported. Low blood sugar is a concern especially with insulin or a sulfonylurea. The label calls for monitoring people with diabetic retinopathy and describes pulmonary aspiration reports during anesthesia or deep sedation.

Hair loss: what the label reports

Hair loss was associated with weight reduction. Rates among women and men were 7.1% and 0.5% with Zepbound versus 1.3% and 0% with placebo.

Lean mass and muscle: separate measurements

In the 160-person SURMOUNT-1 DXA substudy, body weight changed by −21.3% versus −5.3%; fat mass by −33.9% versus −8.2%; and lean mass by −10.9% versus −2.6%. About 75% of lost weight was fat and 25% lean mass in both groups. Grade B.

What longer trials and postmarketing reports can tell us

SURMOUNT trials consistently reported gastrointestinal events as the most common adverse events. Postmarketing reports include pancreatitis, ileus, intestinal obstruction, severe constipation, serious allergy, pulmonary aspiration, and kidney failure. Their frequency cannot be reliably estimated.
