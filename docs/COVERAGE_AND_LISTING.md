## Suggested listing after release testing

**Supplement evidence checks**

Look up reviewed supplement ingredients, see whether a specific claim is covered, and compare a transcribed label with available study details. See the sources, what the researchers measured, and what the label leaves out. Coverage varies by ingredient and outcome.

- What does your catalog say about magnesium glycinate specifically?
- Does the evidence support this ashwagandha label's claim that it lowers cortisol by 30%?
- Can you compare the form and amount on my horsetail label with a human study?
